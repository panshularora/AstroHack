## 🔮 2. Core Breakthrough Features

### 🎙️ A. Live Consultation Room (`/app/room/:id`)
- **Waiting Room & Mode Selection**: Toggle between **Video Call**, **Audio Call**, and **Live Chat** with real-time rate meters (`₹15/min`).
- **Active Streaming & Controls**: Live audio wave visualizer, PIP video stream preview, live session timer, and mic/video mute controls.
- **In-Call Chat & Live Transcript**: Real-time message exchange with astrologers during live sessions.
- **AI Auto-Extraction**: Ending a session automatically triggers an AI parser that extracts new predictions (*Tech Offer 88% Conf.*) and remedy tasks (*Venus Beej Mantra 108 Recitations*) straight into state.

### 🧠 B. Interactive AI Cosmic Twin (`/app/companion`)
- **Dynamic Chart-Aware Intelligence**: Reactive chat engine that analyzes any user prompt (*career, transits, remedies, prediction windows, astrologer recommendations*) against their Leo Sun & Scorpio Ascendant birth chart.
- **Memory Context Citations**: Live citation chips linking directly to past consultation transcripts, active Dasha periods (*Rahu Mahadasha*), and prediction target dates.

### 🎯 C. Prediction Proof Engine (`/app/predictions`)
- **Living Prediction Objects**: Every prediction contains expected time windows, confidence percentages, verification status badges, evidence upload capabilities, and astrologer trust score impact metrics.
- **Outcome Verification**: Allows users to attach real-world proof (offer letters, contract dates) to confirm accuracy over time.

### 📜 D. Lifelong Cosmic Memory & Timeline (`/app/memory` & `/app/journey`)
- **Searchable Memory Vault**: Archived consultations, journal entries, audio notes, and verified milestones accessible via instantaneous search.
- **Life Journey Map**: Visual chapter map tracking major life events from birth through school, career, relationships, and predictions.

### 📊 E. Spotify Wrapped for Life Reports (`/app/reports`)
- **Narrative Life Reports**: Weekly, Monthly, and Annual Wrapped summaries featuring mental clarity vs. transit intensity charts, habit adherence scores, and milestone logs.

### 💖 F. Ashtakoot Kundli Synastry (`/app/relationship`)
- **Relationship Compatibility**: 36-point Ashtakoot Guna Milan analysis (31/36 matched), partner Kundli matching, and commitment timing windows.

### 🛡️ G. Emergency Guidance & SOS Shield (`/app/sos`)
- **Cosmic Grounding**: Interactive 4-second Pranayama breathing exercise, crisis hotline directory, and priority astrologer emergency queue.

## 🎨 3. Handcrafted Architectural Design System

- **Dual Canvas Aesthetics**: Deep Cosmic Midnight (`#080C14`) dark hero fields fading gracefully into an Ivory Editorial Surface (`#F7F5F0`).
- **Typography Scale**:
